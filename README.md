# Riders Performance Analysis Dashboard

A comprehensive Power BI–based analytical dashboard designed to deliver actionable insights into rider activity, driver performance, booking trends, cancellations, and service efficiency across different countries and service types.

## 1. Project Overview 

The **Riders Performance Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive reporting solution developed to analyze key operational metrics of a ride-hailing platform.
It covers the full lifecycle of ride operations, including offers, bookings, cancellations, completed rides, drivers, and service types.

This dashboard helps business stakeholders understand:

Driver availability

Customer booking behavior

Country-wise ride distribution

Service-type performance (TAXI vs PHV)

Seasonal and monthly ride trends

## 2. Purpose & Objectives
**Business Problem**

Ride-hailing platforms generate millions of ride offers and bookings. Without analytics, it becomes difficult to answer important performance questions such as:

How many rides are being offered and completed?

Which service type (TAXI / PHV) is performing better?

What is the cancellation rate and where is it happening more?

How efficient is the offer-to-booking conversion?

How do demand patterns change across months and countries?

**Goal of the Dashboard**

To build a visual, easy-to-understand analytics tool that:

Centralizes driver, booking, cancellation, and ride data

Helps analyze platform efficiency and customer behavior

Highlights service-level and country-level performance

Supports strategy and operational improvement

Provides insights into ride trends, peak seasons, and conversion efficiency

## 3. Tech Stack / Tools Used

📊 Power BI – Main dashboard development

📈 Power BI Visuals – Bar charts, line charts, KPIs, filters

🔍 Slicers & Filters – Interactive data exploration

🔄 Power Query – Data transformation and cleaning

🧮 DAX Measures – KPIs (Bookings, Rides, Cancellations, Conversion Rate)

## 4. Data Source & Table Structure

Source: Private Project (Rideit_drivers & Rideit_drivers_activity)

Bookings

## 7. Business Impact & Insights
Operational Insights:

Identifies high-demand regions

Reveals cancellation hotspots

Helps optimize driver allocation

Shows booking trends across months

Performance Insights:

TAXI service is more efficient than PHV

High gap between offers and bookings shows improvement areas

Germany contributes majority of bookings and rides

Customer Insights:

Seasonal spikes in January–February

Lower conversions in April indicate operational gaps

Better stability in later months

Strategic Insights:

Improve PHV driver supply to reduce cancellations

Strengthen service in ES to improve bookings

Improve matching logic to increase conversion
